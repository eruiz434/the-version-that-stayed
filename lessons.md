# Lessons I Paid For

I wish some of these lessons had come without so much pain, but they didn't.

- Love does not replace boundaries.
- Intensity is not the same thing as security.
- Someone can matter enormously and still not be right for my future.
- Loneliness can make one person carry too many emotional needs.
- I should not make romantic decisions just because I feel alone.
- A person should earn the serious version of me through consistency, reciprocity, safety, and actions that match words.
- I can regret what I did without turning regret into contact.
- I can miss someone without needing them back.
- Detachment is not forgetting. It is when the memory stops asking me to do something.
