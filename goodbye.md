# Goodbye

I wish I had known which goodbye was the real one.

I wish I had understood sooner that you were leaving for good. There are things I would have handled differently if I had known, and there are things I regret.

But I know now.

I don't want to bother you. I don't want to convince you. I don't want to spend the rest of my life negotiating with an ending that already happened.

I loved you.
I wanted a future with you.
It ended.

I am done waiting for a different ending.

Goodbye to the version that stayed.
