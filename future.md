# The Future

The next chapter is not about proving I can replace her.

It is about becoming someone who can be alone without feeling abandoned, who can want closeness without being ruled by loneliness, and who can love someone without making them his entire emotional home.

I want to finish my degree.
I want a better job and a new environment.
I want training, friends, family, purpose, and a life with enough in it that love becomes part of the life rather than the whole thing.

And when I meet someone worth being serious with, I want to meet her as myself — not as a man trying to escape an old heartbreak.
