# Fantasy vs. Real

There is a difference between missing a person and missing what I hoped the relationship would become.

## The fantasy

The version who stayed.
The version who always chose me.
The version who listened to the small details of my day.
The version I could trust with gifts, vulnerability, marriage, kids, and ordinary life.

## The reality

The relationship ended.
She said she did not want to rekindle it.
There were hurt feelings, mistakes, mistrust, and an ending I struggled to understand.

Both can be true:

I loved her deeply.
And I would not feel safe simply stepping back into the relationship I actually had.

Missing the fantasy does not mean I should chase the reality.
