# Memories

Some memories are painful because nothing dramatic happened in them.

- Walking across a small bridge together and looking at the stars.
- Downloading a constellation app because I wanted to know what we were looking at.
- Wanting to tell her ordinary stories after work.
- Small foods, places, roads, and routines becoming reminders long after the relationship ended.
- The feeling of having someone to come home to emotionally.

The memories don't need to be destroyed. They just need to become memories instead of instructions.
